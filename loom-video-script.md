# Loom Video Walkthrough Script

## Project: BFSI Customer Onboarding
**Duration:** 3–4 minutes  
**Audience:** Hiring managers, interviewers, portfolio reviewers

---

### Slide 1: Introduction (30 sec)

> "Hi, I'm Sagar Kandelkar, a Business Analyst specializing in BFSI. In this walkthrough, I'll show you my Customer Onboarding project — a complete case study analyzing how banks can streamline KYC, reduce onboarding time, and improve compliance."

### Slide 2: Problem Statement (45 sec)

> "The current onboarding process takes 7–10 days with 60% manual steps. Key pain points: branch dependency, document resubmission cycles, siloed risk checks, and poor applicant visibility."

Show: gap_analysis.md with AS-IS metrics

### Slide 3: My Approach (45 sec)

> "I approached this by mapping the current state, defining a future state with straight-through processing, and creating a phased roadmap. I built synthetic data, SQL schemas, analytical views, and an interactive dashboard."

Show: process flow diagram, ERD

### Slide 4: Key Findings (60 sec)

> "Key findings: 40% of applications need document resubmission; risk assessment adds 2 days; applicants have zero visibility. My TO-BE design reduces this to under 24 hours with OCR, API-based KYC, and auto-approval for low-risk profiles."

Show: demo-dashboard.html with charts

### Slide 5: Deliverables (30 sec)

> "Deliverables include: requirements document, gap analysis, implementation roadmap, SQL schema with views, Python EDA notebook, process flow diagrams, and this interactive HTML dashboard."

Quick scroll through repo structure

### Slide 6: Closing (15 sec)

> "This project demonstrates my end-to-end BA capabilities — from stakeholder interviews to data modeling to executive-ready dashboards. Thank you for watching."

---

## Recording Tips

- **Tool:** Loom (free) or OBS Studio
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080
- **Audio:** Use a headset or quiet room
- **Cursor:** Highlight what you're showing
- **Pace:** Speak slowly; pause between sections
- **Background:** Clean desktop, no notifications
