# BFSI Product Enhancement — Portfolio Case Study

## Overview

This portfolio case study applies product management lifecycle methodology to banking products — from market research and concept development through MVP launch and iterative enhancement.

**Label:** Portfolio Case Study — Synthetic Scenario

---

## 1. Complete Customer Lifecycle

```
Market Research → Concept Development → Feasibility → Design → Development → Launch → Post-Launch Review
```

| Stage | AS-IS Experience | TO-BE Experience |
|-------|------------------|------------------|
| **Market Research** | Annual industry reports | Continuous competitive intelligence |
| **Concept** | Internal brainstorming | Customer co-creation workshops |
| **Feasibility** | IT-only assessment | Business + tech + compliance triage |
| **Design** | Waterfall specs | Agile user stories + prototypes |
| **Development** | 18-month releases | 4-month MVP cycles |
| **Launch** | Big-bang marketing | Phased beta + iteration |
| **Post-Launch** | Annual review | Continuous feedback integration |

---

## 2. Transformation Impact — AS-IS vs TO-BE

| Metric | Before (AS-IS) | After (TO-BE) | Improvement |
|--------|---------------|---------------|-------------|
| **Time to Market** | 18 months | 4 months | **78% faster** |
| **Feature Success** | 30% adopted | 72% adopted | **2.4x better** |
| **Customer Feedback Loop** | Annual | Continuous | **Always-on** |
| **Product Failure Rate** | 45% | 15% | **67% reduction** |
| **Revenue per Product** | ₹3.2 Cr | ₹7.8 Cr | **2.4x higher** |
| **Customer Co-Creation** | 0% | 25% of features | **Innovation** |
