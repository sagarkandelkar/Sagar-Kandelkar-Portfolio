## Critical Stakeholder Deep Dives

### Head of Branch Ops — Sentiment Journey

| Phase | Sentiment | Why | Intervention Applied |
|-------|-----------|-----|---------------------|
| Strategy | 😐 Neutral | Not consulted initially | Added to steering committee |
| Design | 😐 Neutral | Worried about headcount impact | Headcount-neutral commitment signed |
| Build | 😠 Resistant | Branch dashboard not intuitive | Usability testing + feedback incorporated |
| UAT | 😠 Resistant | Loan officers struggling with new flow | 1:1 coaching sessions + super-user program |
| Rollout | 😐 Neutral | Dashboard improved, but adoption slow | Branch-level leaderboard + recognition |

**Risk:** If sentiment doesn't improve to 😄 by Month 6, branch-level adoption may stall.

### Loan Officers — Sentiment Journey

| Phase | Sentiment | Why | Intervention Applied |
|-------|-----------|-----|---------------------|
| Strategy | ❓ Unknown | Not identified as key stakeholder | Added to stakeholder map |
| Design–Build | 😠 Resistant | Fear of job displacement | "Advisor, not data entry" messaging |
| UAT | 😠 Resistant | System slow during peak hours | Performance tuning + load balancing |
| Rollout | 😐 Neutral | Super-users showing value | Peer mentoring program launched |

**Risk:** Highest resistance group. Requires sustained change management.
