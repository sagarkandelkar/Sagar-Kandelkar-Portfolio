# Credit Risk & Loan Operations — Gap Analysis

## AS-IS Process (Current State)

### Pain Points

| # | Pain Point | Impact |
|---|-----------|--------|
| 1 | Paper-based application with manual entry | Slow processing, data errors |
| 2 | Single CIBIL report only | Incomplete credit picture |
| 3 | Static scorecard with few variables | Inaccurate risk assessment |
| 4 | Credit committee decision (7 days) | Customer drop-off, lost business |
| 5 | Monthly batch portfolio review | Delayed problem identification |

### AS-IS Metrics

| Metric | Current | Target | Gap |
|--------|---------|--------|-----|
| NPA Ratio | 4.2% | 1.8% | 2.4 points |
| Provisioning Accuracy | 72% | 94% | 22 points |
| Recovery Time | 18 months | 8 months | 10 months |
| Credit Decision Time | 10 days | 2 hours | ~10 days |
| Portfolio Yield | 8.5% | 10.2% | 170 bps |

## TO-BE Process (Future State)

### Vision
A fully digital credit risk lifecycle with API-based verification, ML-powered scoring, risk-based auto-approval, real-time portfolio monitoring, and data-driven recovery — maximizing portfolio yield while minimizing NPA.

## Recommendations

### Phase 1: Digital Foundation
- Deploy digital application with auto-fill
- Implement multi-bureau + alternative data APIs
- Launch basic ML scorecard

### Phase 2: Intelligence & Automation
- Deploy advanced PD/LGD models
- Implement risk-based auto-decision engine
- Launch real-time portfolio monitoring

### Phase 3: Recovery & Optimization
- Deploy early warning system
- Launch recovery strategy optimizer
- Automate Basel compliance reporting
