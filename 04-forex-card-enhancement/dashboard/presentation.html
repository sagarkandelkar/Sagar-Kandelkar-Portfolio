Forex Card Enhancement Study

Digital Transformation of Multi-Currency Travel Cards

Portfolio Case Study — Synthetic Data

Problem Statement

  • Branch-only application causes 5-7 day card issuance delays
  • No real-time exchange rate visibility at load time
  • Single-currency cards require multiple cards for multi-country travel
  • Manual LRS tracking creates compliance risk and customer inconvenience
  • Unused balance repatriation takes 15+ days

Approach & Methodology

  • Journey Mapping: Application → Load → Spend → Repatriate → Close
  • Currency Analysis: Track usage across 9 currencies and 11 countries
  • LRS Tracking: Monitor utilization against $250,000 annual limit
  • Digital Gap Assessment: Identify self-service and automation opportunities

Key Findings

  • USD and GBP account for highest transaction volumes — focus rate competitiveness
  • Average LRS utilization is ~38% — room for growth with targeted offers
  • POS dominates transactions, but e-commerce is growing rapidly
  • USA, UK, UAE are top destinations — tailor marketing
  • 10% of cards are blocked — investigate KYC expiry and lost card root causes

Recommendations

  • Launch instant digital application with video KYC
  • Deploy multi-currency wallet supporting 10 currencies
  • Implement rate-lock for 30 minutes during load
  • Add real-time LRS limit alerts at 70%, 85%, 95% thresholds
  • Enable one-click unused balance repatriation to savings account
