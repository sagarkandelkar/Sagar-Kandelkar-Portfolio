# Business Impact & ROI Analysis

## Executive Summary

The Forex Card Digital Enhancement initiative is projected to deliver **₹4.2 Cr annual benefits** against a **₹62 Lakh investment**, achieving a **7.7x ROI** over 24 months. Benefits span cost reduction, revenue growth, risk mitigation, and customer experience improvement.

---

## Investment Breakdown

| Phase | Duration | Investment | Focus Area |
|-------|----------|-----------|------------|
| Phase 1 | Months 1-2 | ₹17 Lakh | Digital Foundation |
| Phase 2 | Months 3-4 | ₹26 Lakh | Multi-Currency & Automation |
| Phase 3 | Months 5-6 | ₹19 Lakh | Intelligence & Self-Service |
| **Total** | **6 months** | **₹62 Lakh** | **Full Digital Transformation** |
