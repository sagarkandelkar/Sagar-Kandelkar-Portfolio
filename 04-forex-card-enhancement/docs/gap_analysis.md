# Forex Card Enhancement — Gap Analysis

## Current State (AS-IS)

### Pain Points

| # | Pain Point | Impact |
|---|-----------|--------|
| 1 | Branch-only application and KYC | Delayed card issuance, customer drop-off |
| 2 | No real-time exchange rate visibility | Customer surprise at conversion rates |
| 3 | Single-currency cards per trip | Multiple cards for multi-country travel |
| 4 | Manual balance repatriation process | Unused funds locked for weeks |
| 5 | No spending visibility during travel | Budget overruns, customer anxiety |
| 6 | LRS tracking done manually by branch | Compliance risk, customer inconvenience |
| 7 | Lost card requires branch visit | Vulnerable period, poor experience |

### AS-IS Metrics

| Metric | Current | Target | Gap |
|--------|---------|--------|-----|
| Card Issuance Time | 5-7 days | <48 hours | Significant |
| Customer Satisfaction | 3.2/5 | >4.2/5 | 1.0 point |
| Digital Transaction % | 15% | >60% | 45% |
| Balance Repatriation Time | 15 days | <3 days | 12 days |
| LRS Compliance Accuracy | 85% | 100% | 15% |

## Future State (TO-BE)

### Vision
A fully digital Forex card experience with multi-currency wallets, real-time rate transparency, instant issuance, and self-service management — delivering convenience, compliance, and confidence to international travelers.

## Recommendations

### Phase 1 (0-2 months): Digital Foundation
- Launch mobile app Forex card module
- Enable digital application with video KYC
- Provide real-time exchange rate display

### Phase 2 (2-4 months): Multi-Currency & Automation
- Deploy multi-currency wallet (up to 10 currencies)
- Implement auto-conversion at POS
- Launch instant transaction alerts

### Phase 3 (4-6 months): Intelligence & Self-Service
- Add spending analytics and budgeting
- Enable one-click balance repatriation
- Implement predictive LRS limit alerts
