# AS-IS vs TO-BE Gap Analysis
## Digital Lead Generation & Consent-Based Outreach Platform

---

## 1. Process Flow Comparison

### AS-IS: Current State
```
Customer searches "personal loan" on Google
    │
    ▼
Clicks bank ad → lands on product page
    │
    ▼
Browses for 2–3 minutes
    │
    ▼
[NO TRACKING — bank is blind]
    │
    ▼
Customer leaves. Intent signal lost forever.
    │
    ▼
2 days later: Bank purchases lead list from broker
    │
    ▼
Tele-caller cold-calls customer
    │
    ▼
Customer annoyed: "I never asked for this"
    │
    ▼
Complaint filed → Brand damage → DND registered
    │
    ▼
Cost per conversion: ₹4,200 | Conversion: 2%
```

### TO-BE: Future State
```
Customer searches "personal loan" on Google
    │
    ▼
Clicks bank ad → lands on product page
    │
    ▼
Web tracker captures: page view, scroll, time, calculator use
    │
    ▼
Exit intent: "Get best rate on WhatsApp?"
    │
    ▼
Customer enters phone + checks consent box
    │
    ▼
Lead scored in real time: Composite 81 (🔥 HOT)
    │
    ▼
Pushed to CRM with consent flags + preferred channel
    │
    ▼
RM calls within 2 hours with pre-approved offer
    │
    ▼
Customer: "Perfect timing — I was just looking!"
    │
    ▼
Application initiated during call
    │
    ▼
Cost per conversion: ₹280 | Conversion: 13%
```

## 2. Gap Analysis Matrix

| Area | AS-IS State | TO-BE State | Gap | Impact | Priority |
|------|-------------|-------------|-----|--------|----------|
| **Web Tracking** | No visitor tracking | Intent tracker captures 9 event types | No visibility into anonymous behavior | High customer acquisition cost | 🔴 Critical |
| **Consent Management** | No consent capture; cold calling | Granular CMP with 6 categories + withdrawal | Regulatory risk + complaints | Fines + brand damage | 🔴 Critical |
| **Lead Scoring** | All leads treated equally | Multi-dimensional composite score (0–100) | Sales wastes time on low-intent leads | Poor productivity | 🔴 Critical |
| **Unified Customer View** | Data in silos | CDP stitches anonymous + known profiles | No single view of customer journey | Incomplete personalization | 🟡 High |
| **Omnichannel Outreach** | Phone-only cold calling | WhatsApp/Email/SMS/Phone based on preference | Low engagement + high annoyance | Low conversion | 🟡 High |
| **Attribution** | Last-click only | Full-funnel from impression to disbursal | Cannot optimize marketing spend | Budget waste | 🟡 High |
| **Compliance Reporting** | Manual spreadsheets | Automated audit-ready reports | Audit prep takes 2 weeks | Regulatory exposure | 🟡 High |
| **Sales SLA Monitoring** | No SLA tracking | Real-time alerts for Hot (2h) / Warm (24h) | Hot leads go cold | Lost revenue | 🟡 High |
| **A/B Testing** | No testing | Banner/message variant testing | Suboptimal UX and conversion | Missed optimization | 🟢 Medium |
| **Predictive Analytics** | Reactive only | ML-driven next-best-product | Cannot anticipate needs | Competitive disadvantage | 🟢 Medium |

## 5. Gap Closure Roadmap

### Phase 1: Foundation (Month 1)
- Deploy web intent tracker on product pages
- Implement cookie consent banner (basic)
- Establish data pipeline to CDP

### Phase 2: Scoring & Routing (Month 2)

- Integrate with Salesforce CRM
- Deploy sales SLA alerts

### Phase 3: Outreach & Personalization (Month 3)
- Launch WhatsApp Business API integration
- Deploy email drip campaigns

- Automated compliance reporting
