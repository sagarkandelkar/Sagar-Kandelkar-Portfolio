# Credit Card Customer Journey — Gap Analysis

## Current State (AS-IS)

### Pain Points

| # | Pain Point | Impact |
|---|-----------|--------|
| 1 | Manual application processing taking 7-10 days | Customer drop-off, competitive disadvantage |
| 2 | No digital activation option | Delayed first usage, poor onboarding |
| 3 | Limited spend visibility for customers | Budget overruns, surprise bills |
| 4 | Complex rewards redemption process | Low redemption rates, engagement drop |
| 5 | Reactive churn management | Customer exits before intervention |
| 6 | No personalized product recommendations | Missed cross-sell opportunities |

### AS-IS Metrics

| Metric | Current | Target | Gap |
|--------|---------|--------|-----|
| Application-to-Activation Time | 12 days | <3 days | 9 days |
| Approval Rate | 75% | >85% | 10% |
| Activation Rate | 60% | >85% | 25% |
| Rewards Redemption Rate | 25% | >60% | 35% |
| Customer Satisfaction | 3.5/5 | >4.3/5 | 0.8 point |
| Churn Rate | 18% | <10% | 8% |

## Future State (TO-BE)

### Vision
A fully digital, AI-powered credit card experience with instant approval, seamless activation, intelligent spend management, and proactive engagement — turning every customer into a loyal, high-value cardholder.

## Recommendations

### Phase 1 (0-2 months): Digital Foundation
- Launch instant approval for pre-qualified customers
- Deploy digital card activation via app
- Implement real-time spend notifications

### Phase 2 (2-4 months): Intelligence & Engagement
- Deploy category-wise spend tracking and budgeting
- Launch one-click rewards redemption
- Implement welcome journey automation

### Phase 3 (4-6 months): Retention & Growth
- Deploy predictive churn models
- Launch personalized limit enhancement offers
- Implement product upgrade recommendations
