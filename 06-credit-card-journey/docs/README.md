# Credit Card Customer Journey Mapping

## Overview

This portfolio case study maps the complete credit card customer lifecycle — from application and approval through activation, usage, rewards redemption, and retention — to identify experience improvement opportunities and process optimization in the BFSI sector.

**Label:** Portfolio Case Study — Synthetic Scenario

## Objectives

1. Map end-to-end credit card customer journey with all touchpoints
2. Identify friction points in application, approval, and activation processes
3. Analyze spending patterns and rewards utilization
4. Define retention risk indicators and intervention strategies
5. Propose digital enhancements for each journey stage

## Key Areas

- **Application:** Digital application, document upload, eligibility check, instant approval
- **Approval & Fulfillment:** Credit bureau check, income verification, card printing, delivery
- **Activation:** Digital activation via app/IVR, PIN setup, first transaction nudges
- **Usage:** Spend patterns, category preferences, payment behavior, limit utilization
- **Rewards & Engagement:** Points accrual, redemption, milestone benefits, engagement campaigns
- **Retention:** Churn signals, win-back campaigns, upgrade paths
