# Insurance Claims — Gap Analysis

## Current State (AS-IS)

### Pain Points

| # | Pain Point | Impact |
|---|-----------|--------|
| 1 | Manual claim registration at branch | Delayed intimation |
| 2 | Physical survey for all claims | High operational cost |
| 3 | No real-time claim status visibility | Customer frustration |
| 4 | Paper-based assessment and approval | Processing delays |
| 5 | Settlement TAT exceeds 10 days | Customer dissatisfaction |

### AS-IS Metrics

| Metric | Current | Target | Gap |
|--------|---------|--------|-----|
| Avg Settlement TAT | 12 days | <7 days | 5 days |
| Digital Intimation | 20% | >80% | 60% |
| Surveyor Productivity | 4 claims/day | >8 claims/day | 4 claims |
| Customer Satisfaction | 3.2/5 | >4.3/5 | 1.1 points |

## Future State (TO-BE)

### Vision
A fully digital claims platform with AI-assisted survey, auto-approval for low-value claims, and real-time customer communication — delivering seamless claim experiences.

## Recommendations

### Phase 1 (0-2 months): Digital Intimation
- Launch mobile app claim registration
- Deploy WhatsApp claim bot
- Implement photo-based auto-triage

### Phase 2 (2-4 months): Smart Survey
- Video survey for claims < ₹50K
- AI damage assessment from photos
- Auto-survey for clear-cut cases

### Phase 3 (4-6 months): Auto-Settlement
- Straight-through processing for < ₹50K
- Digital approval workflow
- Real-time settlement tracking
