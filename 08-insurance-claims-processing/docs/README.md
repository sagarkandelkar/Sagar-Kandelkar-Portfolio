# Insurance Claims Processing Analysis

## Overview

This portfolio case study analyzes the complete insurance claims lifecycle — from intimation and registration through survey, assessment, approval, and settlement — to identify process improvement opportunities and customer experience enhancements in the BFSI sector.

**Label:** Portfolio Case Study — Synthetic Scenario

## Objectives

1. Map the end-to-end claims processing workflow
2. Identify bottlenecks in survey, assessment, and approval stages
3. Define digital enhancement opportunities (AI survey, straight-through claims)
4. Propose a roadmap for claims automation and fraud detection
5. Demonstrate business analysis skills in insurance operations

## Key Areas

- **Claim Intimation:** Digital intimation, document upload, policy verification
- **Registration:** Claim ID generation, coverage validation, TPA assignment
- **Survey & Assessment:** Damage evaluation, estimate preparation, approval workflow
- **Settlement:** Payment calculation, disbursement, recovery (if subrogation)
- **Fraud Detection:** Anomaly detection, pattern analysis, investigation workflows
