Fraud Detection & Risk Analytics

Real-Time Monitoring, Rule-Based Alerting & Pattern Analysis

Portfolio Case Study — Synthetic Data

Problem Statement

  • Reactive fraud detection leads to irreversible financial losses
  • High false positive rates create customer friction
  • Siloed data across channels prevents unified risk scoring
  • Manual alert triage causes delayed investigation response

Approach & Methodology

  • Multi-Factor Risk Scoring: Amount, geography, time, device, velocity
  • Rule Engine: Configurable thresholds with versioning support
  • Alert Triage: Prioritize by severity and auto-route to teams
  • Pattern Analysis: Off-hours, geo-anomaly, velocity triggers

Key Findings

  • 28% fraud rate in monitored transactions — targeted monitoring critical
  • Fraudulent transactions have avg risk score of 85 vs 35 for genuine
  • Off-hours (10 PM – 5 AM) show elevated fraud rates
  • UPI and Internet Banking carry highest online fraud risk
  • Velocity rules are the most frequent alert trigger

Recommendations

  • Implement dynamic thresholds for transactions > ₹50,000 online
  • Apply stricter velocity limits during off-hours
  • Deploy step-up authentication for high-risk score transactions
  • Focus rule tuning on velocity and off-hours patterns
  • Build ML fraud prediction model for Phase 3 enhancement
