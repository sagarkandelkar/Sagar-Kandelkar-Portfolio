# Remittance & International Transfers — Gap Analysis

## AS-IS Process (Current State)

### Pain Points

| # | Pain Point | Impact |
|---|-----------|--------|
| 1 | No real-time tracking | Customer anxiety, support calls |
| 2 | Hidden FX margins | Customer distrust, complaints |
| 3 | Manual compliance checks | Delays, regulatory risk |
| 4 | 3-5 day settlement | Cash flow issues for recipients |
| 5 | Opaque correspondent fees | Surprise deductions |

### AS-IS Metrics

| Metric | Current | Target | Gap |
|--------|---------|--------|-----|
| Transfer Time | 3-5 days | < 24 hours | 4+ days |
| FX Margin | 3.5% | 1.2% | 2.3 points |
| Failed Transfers | 8% | 2% | 6 points |
| Customer Satisfaction | 2.9/5 | 4.3/5 | 1.4 points |

## TO-BE Process (Future State)

A fully transparent international transfer platform with real-time SWIFT gpi tracking, competitive FX rates, automated compliance, and instant settlement — making remittances as easy as domestic payments.

| Benefit | Impact |

| Cost Transparency | Full visibility |
