# Bank Account Lifecycle — Gap Analysis

## AS-IS Process (Current State)

### Pain Points

| # | Pain Point | Impact |
|---|-----------|--------|
| 1 | Reactive dormancy management | Revenue loss, customer churn |
| 2 | No cross-sell intelligence | Low product penetration |
| 3 | Siloed account information | Poor customer experience |
| 4 | Manual closure process | Long TAT, compliance risk |
| 5 | No lifecycle revenue tracking | Missed optimization |

### AS-IS Metrics

| Metric | Current | Target | Gap |
|--------|---------|--------|-----|
| Dormancy Rate | 22% | 9% | 13 points |
| Products per Customer | 2.1 | 3.8 | 1.7 |
| Cross-Sell Success | 12% | 34% | 22 points |
| Closure TAT | 15 days | 3 days | 12 days |

## TO-BE Process (Future State)

### Vision
A proactive account lifecycle management system that predicts dormancy, recommends next-best-products, and maximizes customer lifetime value through personalized engagement.

## Expected Benefits

| Benefit | Impact |
|---------|--------|
| Dormancy Reduction | 59% lower |
| Revenue per Customer | +75% |
| Cross-Sell Success | +22 points |
| Customer Retention | +61% |
