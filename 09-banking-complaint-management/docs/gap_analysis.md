# Banking Complaint Management — Gap Analysis

## AS-IS Process (Current State)

### Pain Points

| # | Pain Point | Impact |
|---|-----------|--------|
| 1 | Manual complaint logging at branch | Delayed registration, data entry errors |
| 2 | No centralized tracking system | Complaints lost between departments |
| 3 | Paper-based categorization | Incorrect routing, long resolution times |
| 4 | No SLA monitoring | Regulatory breaches, customer dissatisfaction |
| 5 | Reactive resolution | Same issues recurring, no prevention |

### AS-IS Metrics

| Metric | Current | Target | Gap |
|--------|---------|--------|-----|
| Resolution TAT | 12 days | 2 days | 10 days |
| First Contact Resolution | 35% | 72% | 37 points |
| Customer Satisfaction | 2.8/5 | 4.2/5 | 1.4 points |
| Repeat Complaints | 28% | 8% | 20 points |

## TO-BE Process (Future State)

### Vision
A fully digital complaint management system with omnichannel intake, AI-powered categorization, real-time SLA tracking, and proactive root cause analytics — delivering faster resolution and higher customer satisfaction.

## Recommendations

### Phase 1: Digital Foundation
- Deploy unified complaint portal
- Implement auto-categorization with NLP
- Launch real-time SLA dashboard

### Phase 2: Intelligence & Automation
- Enable knowledge-based resolution suggestions
- Automate customer communications
- Deploy root cause analytics

### Phase 3: Optimization & Compliance
- Integrate with RBI Ombudsman
- Implement predictive complaint prevention
- Launch customer satisfaction closed-loop
