Digital Payments Process Analysis

Failure Reduction, Settlement Optimization & Customer Experience

Portfolio Case Study — Synthetic Data

Problem Statement

  • High UPI failure rate during peak hours (11% below target)
  • No intelligent retry — permanent failures that could recover
  • Merchant settlement delays beyond T+1 cause cash flow issues
  • Limited failure root cause visibility — reactive maintenance
  • No customer-side failure recovery — high support ticket volume

Approach & Methodology

  • Success Rate Analysis: By payment method (UPI, Wallet, Card, NetBanking)
  • Failure Classification: Categorize by root cause and recovery potential
  • Settlement Tracking: Compare actual vs expected settlement timelines
  • Merchant Performance: Volume, chargeback rate, and status analysis

Key Findings

  • Overall success rate: 80% — needs improvement to >98%
  • Bank timeouts and insufficient funds are top failure reasons
  • E-commerce and Travel merchants drive highest volumes
  • NetBanking has longest settlement cycles but highest success rate
  • UPI preferred by high-LTV customers — invest in UPI reliability

Recommendations

  • Deploy smart gateway routing with health checks and fallback
  • Implement pre-transaction balance validation for wallets and accounts
  • Add automatic retry with exponential backoff for network failures
  • Proactively notify customers of expired cards 30 days before expiry
  • Build merchant settlement tracking portal with delay alerts
