## 🎯 The Problem

```
Before (AS-IS)                          After (TO-BE)
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
12 avg touchpoints                      3 avg touchpoints
3–5 days onboarding TAT                 15 minutes digital TAT
47% drop-off at branch visit            12% drop-off (digital-native)
40% forms have manual errors            5% error rate (auto-fill)
Paper KYC + physical signature          eKYC + eSign
Branch-dependent process                Mobile-first, anytime
```

## 🛠️ Solution Components

| Component | What It Does | Tech |
|-----------|--------------|------|
| **eKYC Gateway** | PAN + Aadhaar real-time validation | NSDL + UIDAI APIs |
| **Document AI** | Auto-classify, verify, and extract KYC docs | OCR + ML model |
| **Risk Scoring Engine** | Auto-tag customer risk (low/med/high) | Rule-based + ML |
| **Product Recommender** | Suggest FD, credit card, insurance at onboarding | Collaborative filtering |
| **eSign Integration** | Paperless agreement execution | eMudhra / SignDesk |

## ⚠️ Risks & Mitigation

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| UIDAI API downtime | Medium | High | Fallback to paper KYC with SLA guarantee |
| Branch resistance to digital | Medium | Medium | Change champions + incentive alignment |
| Elderly customer digital exclusion | High | Medium | Assisted digital counters + IVR fallback |
| Data privacy compliance | Low | Critical | RBI consultation + DPDP Act readiness |
