🎯 The Problem
Current state: Customers visit branches twice, submit photocopies, wait 5 days for account opening. 35% abandon mid-application. Manual KYC errors trigger compliance issues.
💡 The Solution
Digital-first onboarding: Video KYC + OCR auto-validation + real-time AML screening. Single mobile app journey. Automated account creation in under 10 minutes.
⚡ Key Transformation Levers

Before (AS-IS)

  • Branch visit required for KYC
  • Paper forms, manual data entry
  • Overnight AML batch processing
  • 7-day welcome kit delivery
  • ₹2,800 cost per onboarding

After (TO-BE)

  • 3-minute Video KYC from home
  • OCR auto-extracts document data
  • Real-time sanctions screening
  • Digital welcome kit in 60 seconds
  • ₹450 cost per onboarding
