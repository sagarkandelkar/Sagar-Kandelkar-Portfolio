Retail Analytics

Customer Segmentation, Sales Optimization & Campaign ROI

Portfolio Case Study — Synthetic Data

Problem Statement

  • Retailers lack visibility into customer value and churn risk
  • Sales trends across categories and channels are unclear
  • Marketing campaign effectiveness is not measured
  • Inventory decisions are intuition-driven rather than data-driven

Approach & Methodology

  • RFM Analysis: Segment customers by Recency, Frequency, Monetary scores
  • Sales Trend Analysis: Monthly and category-wise revenue tracking
  • Campaign ROI: Compare revenue vs budget for each campaign
  • Product Performance: Rank products by revenue and margin

Key Findings

  • Champions (30%): Recent, frequent, high-value customers — retain with loyalty
  • Loyal Customers (35%): Consistent buyers — upsell and cross-sell
  • At Risk (35%): Declining engagement — re-engage with targeted offers
  • Electronics & Clothing drive 65% of total revenue
  • Summer Sale generated highest ROI at 250%

Recommendations

  • Launch personalized campaigns for each RFM segment
  • Increase inventory for Electronics and Clothing categories
  • Implement automated win-back emails for At-Risk customers
  • Use UPI-first checkout to reduce payment friction
  • A/B test campaign creatives before full rollout
